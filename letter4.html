Ma Lettre d'Amour pour Toi

Flora, this month I’ve really enjoyed saying “I love you” often, followed by your name, Flora. It feels like a way to reaffirm that I’m speaking to you—someone so special. A curious fact is that at the beginning of our relationship, I was afraid to say “I love you” because I thought you might not feel the same way yet, or at least not as deeply as I did. But since you visited me in Malta, I haven’t been afraid to put my feelings for you into words. This month, I missed you even more than in previous ones. I keep falling more in love with you, and I don’t know how that’s even possible—but that’s the beautiful thing about being with you. You constantly amaze me with all the wonderful things you bring into my life. Tomorrow, I’ll finally get to hug you and give you some kisses that will reaffirm everything I’ve said and felt. What I loved most about this month, though, was your desire to marry me in June. I know it’s been stressful for you, but look—at first, I didn’t want to get married until I had achieved a few things, like mastering French and saving more money. But now, my faith and love for you have made me braver because having you by my side has brought light and meaning to my life. I love you so much, Flora, and I hope to also love your family deeply.

I’m so excited for the many anniversaries that await us.
