Ma Lettre d'Amour pour Toi

Amor de mi Vida, just three months ago our souls crossed paths, and the moment I heard your voice, I knew it was you. I’m still amazed that in just one week, you decided to make me a part of your life, and since then, every single day has been a gift to me. With you, I’ve discovered emotions more intense than anything I’ve ever experienced before. Thank you for the blessing of your faith, wisdom, magical powers, and your heart.

I’m so excited for the many anniversaries that await us. I love you more than words can ever express!
